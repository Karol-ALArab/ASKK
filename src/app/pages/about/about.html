JM Woodworking, founded in 2019 by passionate woodworker Joshua Minnis, is based in Windsor and specializes in handcrafted custom wooden products.

Every project is built with precision, premium materials, and exceptional craftsmanship. From custom furniture to charcuterie boards and home décor, every piece is designed with attention to detail.

With outstanding customer reviews and years of experience, JM Woodworking continues to create custom pieces that combine functionality with timeless woodworking techniques.
