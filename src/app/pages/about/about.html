ABOUT THE CRAFTSMAN

MADE BY JM WOODWORKING

JM Woodworking, founded in 2019 by passionate woodworker Joshua Minnis, is based in Windsor and is known for custom wooden products.

The company prides itself on exceptional craftsmanship and personalized service, offering a diverse range of high-quality items. With outstanding reviews, JM Woodworking emphasizes its commitment to artistry and dedication to the community.
